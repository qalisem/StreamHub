# StreamHub User Guide

StreamHub lets you browse movies and shows, choose a plan, and watch. This guide covers subscribers, content managers, and admins.

## Getting started
1. Go to **Sign up**, enter your email, a password (8+ characters with a letter and a number), and a display name. You get a welcome email.
2. Go to **Plans** and pick Basic, Standard, or Premium. Each plan unlocks titles at its tier and below.
3. Enter a card. In the demo, use `4242 4242 4242 4242` with any future date and any 3 digit CVV.

**Forgot your password?** On the login page, choose **Forgot password?** and enter your email. The email contains a link that works once and expires after 30 minutes. In the demo, an admin can read the email under **Emails**.

## Watching
- **Browse** shows the catalog. Search by title or cast, filter by genre, type, year, and rating, and sort.
- Open a title to see its details. Movies have a **Play** button.
- **Shows** list their episodes by season. Pick a season tab and press **Play** on an episode. Your place is saved, so the button changes to **Resume**.
- **Continue Watching** on the home page shows what you started, with the episode for shows.
- **+ My List** saves a title for later. Find it under **My List**.
- Rate a title from 1 to 5 stars and write a review on its page. You can edit or delete your review.

## Your account
- **Account** shows your plan, status, and renewal date. Change plan (upgrades apply now, downgrades at renewal) or cancel (you keep access until the end of the period).
- **Billing history** lists every payment with a printable receipt.
- If a renewal payment fails, your plan becomes **Past Due** and playback stops until you enter a working card on the Account page.
- **Emails you receive:** welcome, renewal reminder 3 days before each renewal, payment failed, cancellation (with your last day of access), plan ended, and password reset or changed.

## Content managers
- **Manage titles**: add, edit, and remove titles. When editing a title, use **Upload poster** to add a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image up to 2 MB.
- On a show's page, use **Add an episode** (season, episode number, name, runtime) and **Remove** next to an episode.
- Remove inappropriate reviews with **Remove** under the review.

## Admins
Everything content managers can do, plus:
- **Manage users**: search users, change roles, deactivate or reactivate accounts, extend or cancel a subscription, and change plan prices.
- **Emails**: read every email the system has sent, newest first. Click a row to see the full message.

## Help
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Choose a plan to watch this title" | You have no plan, or it is Past Due. Go to Plans or Account. |
| "Standard plan required" | The title needs a higher plan. Upgrade on the Account page. |
| Reset link says it is invalid or expired | Request a new link. Each link works once, for 30 minutes. |
| Poster upload refused | Use a PNG, JPEG, or WebP file of 2 MB or less. |
